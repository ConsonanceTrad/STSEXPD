<b>SPSEXPD</b> is a traditional roguelike dungeon crawler RPG: a source-level port of Special Surprise Pixel Dungeon (SPS) 0.9.8 onto the Shattered Pixel Dungeon 4.0.0 engine. It is a distinct project from SPSSPD, the original SPS port. Every game is a unique challenge with randomized levels and enemies, and over a thousand items to collect and use.

<h2><b><u>Long Adventure, Many Heroes</u></b></h2>
Fight through a 26-depth main quest across five regions, with 12 hero classes from SPS's heritage — each with their own gear, skills, and playstyle.

<h2><b><u>Low-Pressure by Design</u></b></h2>
SPSEXPD blends selected low-pressure quality-of-life features of SPS into the Shattered base: spacious backpack and bag storage, generous equipment slots (five universal accessory slots plus a badge slot), dual weapons and armor, and forgiving convenience systems that take the sting out of a bad roll.

<h2><b><u>Open Development</u></b></h2>
SPSEXPD is under active development on top of the Shattered 4.0.0 engine. It is GPLv3, built on Shattered Pixel Dungeon by 00-Evan and Special Surprise Pixel Dungeon by hmdzl.
